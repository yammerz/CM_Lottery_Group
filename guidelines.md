### Guidelines
<div id="guidelines">

- All members must contribute money to the lottery pool by the specified deadline as agreed upon.
- Members will be added to the group only after agreeing to abide by the group's rules and guidelines. 
- By paying their contribution, they acknowledge and accept these terms.
- Members can get general information about the group from this website.
- The trustee will maintain transparent records of all transactions.

</div>

### Game
<div id="game">

- The group will participate in the Lotto Max plus Extra game.
- Lotto Max plays cost $6 each and includes 4 lines per play. 
- Lotto Max Extra plays cost $1 each and include 1 line per play.
- The contributions will be used to purchase the best combination of plays for Jackpot and Extras.
    - Example: If the group has collected $120 in contributions for a draw and each Lotto Max play costs $6, the trustee can purchase 18 plays 6x18 = $108, with 12 of the 18 tickets playing the Lotto Max Extra game.

- <a target="_blank" href="https://www.playnow.com/lottery/lotto-max/">Lotto Max More Information</a>

</div>

### Contributions

<div id="contributions">

- A $6 dollar minimum contribution is required from each member for each draw.
- Members can choose to contribute more than the minimum amount if they wish. The prize money will still be distributed based on each member's individual contribution.
- There are 2 draws per week. One on Tuesday and one on Friday.
- Members will pay in advance for a period of 4 weeks. Example: Total of $6 x 2 x 4 = $48
- Members must make their contributions before the deadline specified for the period, and before any draw takes place.
- There will be no refunds for contributions once they have been made.
- Members who fail to contribute by the deadline will be excluded from the draw for that period and will not be eligible for any winnings from that draw.
- if prize money is in play, the total contributions for that draw will be divided among the members based on their individual contributions.
- New members see section below.
- Contributions call be made via etransfer or by cash. If you use cash, ensure that you get a sms message as reciept.
</div>

### Prize Money

<div id="prize-money">

- Prize money will be distributed among the members based on their individual contributions for each draw.
- If a member fails to contribute for a particular draw, they will not be eligible for any winnings from that draw.
- The formula for distributing winnings is: personal contribution / total contributions per draw.
    - Example 1: If the total contributions for a draw are $120 and a member contributed $6, their share of the winnings would be $6 / $120 = 1/20th.
    - Example 2: a new member has joined the group while prize money is in play;
    we have $120 + $6 from the new member of base contributions and $200 of prize money. The new member receives 6/320 = 1/53.33 of the total winnings.

</div>

### New Members
<div id="new-members">

- New members represent a possible challenge to the existing group as a portion of the previous groups winnings are replayed.
- If a new member joins the group while prize money is in play, they will not be entitled to any portion of the winnings from the draws that occurred before their participation.
- Again the formula for distributing winnings will be contribution / total contributions per draw.
</div>
