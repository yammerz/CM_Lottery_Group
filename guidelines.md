 ## Information and Guidelines

### Guidelines

### Game

<div id="game">
    <ul>
    <li>The group will participate in the Lotto Max plus Extra game.</li>
    <li>Lotto Max plays cost $6 each and includes 4 lines per play.</li>
    <li>Lotto Max Extra plays cost $1 each and include 1 line per play.</li>
    <li>The contributions will be used to purchase the best combination of plays for Jackpot and Extras.
        <ul>
            <li>Example: If the group has collected $120 in contributions for a draw and each Lotto Max play costs $6, the trustee can purchase 18 plays 6x18 = $108, with 12 of the 18 tickets playing the Lotto Max Extra game.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li><a target="_blank" href="https://www.playnow.com/lottery/lotto-max/">Lotto Max More Information</a></li>
    </ul>
</div>

### Contributions

<div id="contributions">
    <ul>
    <li>A $6 dollar minimum contribution is required from each member for each draw.</li>
    <li>Members can choose to contribute more than the minimum amount if they wish. The prize money will still be distributed based on each member's individual contribution.</li>
    <li>There are 2 draws per week. One on Tuesday and one on Friday.</li>
    <li>Members will pay in advance for a period of 4 weeks. Example: Total of $6 x 2 x 4 = $48</li>
    <li>Members must make their contributions before the deadline specified for the period, and before any draw takes place.</li>
    <li>There will be no refunds for contributions once they have been made.</li>
    <li>Members who fail to contribute by the deadline will be excluded from the draw for that period and will not be eligible for any winnings from that draw.</li>
    <li>if prize money is in play, the total contributions for that draw will be divided among the members based on their individual contributions.</li>
    <li>New members see section below.</li>
    <li>Contributions can be made via etransfer or by cash. If you use cash, ensure that you get a sms message as reciept.</li>
    </ul>
</div>

### Prize Money (Winnings Distribution or Share)

<div id="prize-money">
    <ul>
    <li>Prize money will be distributed among the members based on their individual contributions for each draw.
    <li>If a member fails to contribute for a period, they will not be eligible for any winnings from the draws in that period.</li>
    <li>The formula for distributing winnings is: personal contribution / total contributions per draw.</li>
    <li>Example 1: If the total contributions for a draw are $120 and a member contributed $6, their share of the winnings would be $6 / $120 = 1/20th.</li>
    <li>Example 2: a new member has joined the group while prize money is in play;
    we have $120 + $6 from the new member of base contributions and $200 of prize money. The new member receives 6/320 = 1/53.33 of the total winnings.</li>
    </li>
    </ul>
</div>

### New Members

<div id="new-members">
    <ul>
    <li>New members represent a possible challenge to the existing group as a portion of the previous groups winnings are replayed.</li>
    <li>If a new member joins the group while prize money is in play, they will not be entitled to any portion of the winnings from the draws that occurred before their participation.</li>
    <li>Again the formula for distributing winnings will be contribution / total contributions per draw.</li>
    </ul>
</div>
